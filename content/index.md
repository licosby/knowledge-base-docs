## Obsessive Christmas Disorder

Obsessive Christmas Disorder is a holiday knowledge base focused on the history, traditions, décor, and cultural quirks of Christmas. Each section explores a different part of the season, from the origins of Christmas trees and Santa Claus to global celebrations, gift‑giving customs, and the evolution of holiday décor. It’s built for anyone who loves learning the “why” behind Christmas and enjoys discovering fun facts and festive history.
