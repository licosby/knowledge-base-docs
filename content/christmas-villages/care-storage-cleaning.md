# Care, Storage & Cleaning

Christmas village pieces are small, detailed, and often fragile, so a little care goes a long way. Proper cleaning and storage help keep paint bright, lights working, and accessories intact for many years. Whether your village is Lemax, Department 56, DIY, or a mix of everything, these basic routines keep your collection looking its best.

---

## Cleaning Your Village Pieces

### Dusting
Most village pieces only need gentle dusting throughout the season. Soft brushes work well for reaching tiny details without scratching paint or resin.

Good tools include:
- Soft makeup brushes  
- Small paintbrushes  
- Microfiber cloths  
- Compressed air (used lightly and at a distance)

Avoid rough cloths or anything abrasive this can make the paint chip easily.

### Deep Cleaning
If a piece needs more than dusting:
- Use a slightly damp cloth on large, smooth surfaces  
- Avoid soaking or spraying water directly  
- Keep moisture away from electrical components  
- Let pieces dry fully before plugging them in

For stubborn spots, a cotton swab lightly dampened with water works well.

---

## Caring for Lights & Moving Parts

Animated pieces and lighted buildings need a little extra attention:
- Check cords for wear before each season  
- Keep moving parts free of dust  
- Store adapters separately to prevent bending  
- Avoid running animations for long periods if they feel warm or strained  
- Keep replacement bulbs and adapters labeled by brand

If a piece starts making unusual noise, it may need lubrication or internal cleaning. There are professional repair services for this.

---

## Storage Tips

### Packing Materials
Use materials that protect without scratching:
- Acid‑free tissue paper  
- Bubble wrap (not directly on painted surfaces)  
- Foam sheets  
- Original packaging when available  

Avoid newspaper as the ink can transfer to paint.

### Boxing & Labeling
Store pieces in sturdy bins or boxes:
- Label each box by village series or theme  
- Keep heavy items at the bottom  
- Wrap accessories separately  
- Store cords and adapters in small labeled bags

---

## Seasonal Setup & Take‑Down

### Setup
- Place animated pieces on stable surfaces  
- Keep cords organized with clips or ties  
- Use risers to create height and protect fragile pieces  
- Avoid placing heavy items near delicate figurines  

### Take‑Down
- Let animated pieces cool before packing  
- Remove batteries from any battery‑powered items  
- Wrap each piece individually  
- Check for damage before storing so repairs aren’t forgotten

---

## Long‑Term Preservation
Collectors who display villages for many years often:
- Rotate pieces to reduce wear  
- Keep original boxes for safe storage  
- Use low‑heat LED lighting  
- Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading  

Small habits like these help villages stay bright and beautiful for decades.
