# Christmas Village

Christmas villages bring together miniature houses, figurines, lights, and scenery to create a festive world in small scale. Whether your display is nostalgic, animated, handmade, or a mix of everything, this category covers the major brands, DIY techniques, care tips, and creative ideas that help bring a village to life.

---
## Main Village Brands

### Lemax
Bright colors, animated pieces, moving figurines, and playful seasonal themes. Lemax villages feel lively and energetic, with a wide range of accessories and affordable options.

### Department 56
Classic, detailed, and nostalgic. Department 56 focuses on craftsmanship, traditional holiday scenes, and iconic collections such as Dickens Village and Snow Village.

## DIY & Creative Builds

### DIY Village
Guides for creating your own houses, terrain, snow, water features, and props using materials like cardstock, clay, foam, and 3D printing. Perfect for customizing your display.

Tips for keeping village pieces dust‑free, protected, and safely stored between seasons. Includes cleaning methods, packing materials, and long‑term preservation ideas.
