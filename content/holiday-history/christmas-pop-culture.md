# 🎄 First Recorded Christmas Celebration

Christmas was not originally Christmas as we know it today. December 25 was celebrated at the return of longer days. Today's secular part of Christmas still follows the roots of Saturnalia with a festival of family gatherings, feasting, and gift exchanging. Constantine aligned the holiday with Christianity around 336 AD, declaring it the celebration of Jesus’s birth and transforming it into the religious holiday we know today.[^first-christmas]

# 🌲 Famous Christmas Trees

## Rockefeller Center Tree
- First tree: 1931  
- Height: 69–100 ft  
- Lights: 50,000 LEDs
- Typically a Norway Spruce tree

The tradition of setting up the tree in the Rockefeller Center plaza dates back to 1933. Since 1951, the lighting ceremony of the typical Norway spruce has been broadcast live on television, becoming over time an international event.[^rockefeller-tree]

## Biggest Christmas Trees in the World
- Gubbio, Italy: 650 meters tall  
- 3,000 lights  
- 8.5 km of cables  

Gubbio, Italy has held the Guinness World Record for the world’s biggest Christmas tree since 1991.[^gubbio-record]
