- “Jingle Bells” was originally written as a **Thanksgiving** song.  
- “Do They Know It’s Christmas?” by Band Aid has raised more than £200 million since its release and originally raised £8 million ($28+ million USD). Investigations later revealed that much of the aid was diverted by the ruling dictatorship and used to purchase weapons.[^spin-live-aid]

# 🎄 First Recorded Christmas Celebration

Christmas didn’t begin as the holiday we know today. December 25 was originally celebrated as the return of longer days. Many of today’s secular traditions — feasting, gathering with family, exchanging gifts — trace back to Saturnalia. Around 336 AD, Constantine aligned the date with Christianity, declaring it the celebration of Jesus’s birth and shaping the religious holiday recognized today.[^first-christmas]

# 🌲 Famous Christmas Trees

## Rockefeller Center Tree
- First tree: 1931  
- Height: 69–100 ft  
- Lights: 50,000 LEDs  
- Typically a Norway spruce  

The Rockefeller Center tree tradition began in 1933. Since 1951, the lighting ceremony has been broadcast live, turning it into an international holiday event.[^rockefeller-tree]

## Biggest Christmas Trees in the World
- **Gubbio, Italy:** 650 meters tall  
- 3,000 lights  
- 8.5 km of cables  

Gubbio has held the Guinness World Record for the world’s biggest Christmas tree since 1991.[^gubbio-record]
