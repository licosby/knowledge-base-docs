## Holiday History

### What is this page?

This page explains where our favorite holiday traditions came from and how they developed over time. It covers the history behind Christmas decorations, gift giving, Christmas villages, and present presentation. You will learn why certain customs became popular, how different traditions spread, and how they connect to the ways we celebrate today. This section gives you the background knowledge that supports the ideas, tips, and examples found throughout the rest of the project.
