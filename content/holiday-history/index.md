## Holiday History

### What is this page?

This section explains where our favorite holiday traditions came from and how they developed over time. It covers the history behind Christmas decorations, gift giving, Christmas villages, and present presentation. You’ll learn why certain customs became popular, how different traditions spread, and how they connect to the ways we celebrate today.

Think of this page as the foundation for the rest of your holiday project — the background knowledge that supports the ideas, tips, and examples found throughout all the other sections.
